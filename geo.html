The Shift

Search did not stop being SEO. It became harder to earn trust.

AI search still depends on classic search signals: crawlability, relevance, authority, helpful content, and a technically sound site. The difference is that the result may now be a synthesized answer instead of a ranked list. That raises the bar for clarity, proof, and topical depth.

Type 1 — Find a Business

Local SEO still carries the load

  • plumber near me
  • kitchen designer Chicago IL
  • hvac company phone number

The buyer knows what they want and needs a location, phone number, review set, or quick shortlist. Google Business Profile, reviews, proximity, service coverage, and normal local SEO are still the foundation.

Type 2 — Research & Consideration

AI search changes the research journey

  • what to know before hiring a contractor
  • semi-custom vs fully custom cabinets
  • how long does a kitchen remodel take

The buyer is comparing options, risks, costs, timelines, and fit. AI search may answer the first question and then fan out into related questions. The opportunity is to become the source that shapes that consideration set.

For businesses that sell high-consideration products and services — custom work, specialty products, professional services, complex ecommerce categories — the pipeline is often built in Type 2. GEO is highest value when it improves those research pages without weakening the commercial pages that still need to convert.

How We Work

Eight principles for modern SEO in AI search

There is no special trick that guarantees an AI citation. The work is more durable than that: make the page useful enough for people, structured enough for retrieval, and evidenced enough for an answer engine to trust.

Principle 01 — Highest Priority

Keep the SEO foundation intact

GEO does not rescue weak SEO. Crawlable pages, clean internal links, fast usable layouts, descriptive titles, local signals, schema where appropriate, and genuinely helpful content all still matter because AI search is built on search.

Principle 02 — Highest Priority

Answer first, then elaborate

A research page should make its answer clear early, then support it with details. That helps buyers decide whether the page is useful, and it gives answer systems a cleaner summary to understand, cite, or paraphrase.

Principle 03 — Highest Priority

Map the query fan-out

One AI-search prompt can trigger several related searches: cost, comparison, timeline, risk, materials, process, alternatives, and local fit. We map that question tree so your content covers the whole decision, not just one keyword.

Principle 04 — Highest Priority

Replace vague claims with proof

"Trusted quality" is not enough. Specific credentials, named materials, years in business, project counts, service areas, standards, photos, constraints, and real outcomes give both humans and answer systems something to verify.

Principle 05 — High Leverage

Add information a generic AI cannot invent

The best GEO content has information gain: firsthand expertise, actual job conditions, pricing context, product tradeoffs, original photos, owner perspective, and details from sales calls or support questions.

Principle 06 — High Leverage

Structure pages for extraction, not gimmicks

Short answer blocks, comparison tables, decision criteria, FAQs, project facts, and clear headings make complex pages easier to understand. The point is not to write for bots; it is to make expert content easier to reuse accurately.

Principle 07 — High Leverage

Build entity trust on and off the site

Answer engines look for corroboration. Reviews, association pages, manufacturer listings, trade publications, local mentions, partner references, and consistent business data can all strengthen the case that your site deserves to be cited.

Principle 08 — Build Toward

Measure, refresh, and keep learning

AI visibility shifts quickly. We track Search Console signals where available, GA4 referral patterns, branded demand, assisted conversions, and recurring answer checks, then refresh content as buyer questions and search surfaces change.

What GEO Is Not

The best GEO looks like disciplined SEO, not a shortcut

A lot of "AI search optimization" advice is just old SEO spam with new vocabulary. We avoid the gimmicks and focus on changes that make pages genuinely more useful, more specific, and easier to trust.

Not prompt-stuffing

We do not add awkward phrases like "ChatGPT should cite us" or write hidden instructions to answer engines. That is noise, not strategy.

Not schema theater

Schema can reinforce clear content, but it cannot make weak content authoritative. We use structured data only where it matches the real page.

Not generic AI copy

Pages that sound like everyone else are easy to ignore. We look for real expertise, real examples, and the details a generic model would not know.

Not traffic at any cost

AI answers may reduce some clicks. The goal is to earn presence in the answer, shape buyer trust earlier, and win the qualified visits that still matter.

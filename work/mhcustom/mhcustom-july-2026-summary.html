Overview

July brought 572 US sessions, -12.8% from June's 656. Lead-form conversion volume was 23 GA4 conversions, down from 29 in June.

The strongest channel was Organic Search with 282 sessions. Search quality stayed healthy: Google Search Console recorded 159 US clicks and 23,659 impressions, with top query demand still led by brand terms and kitchen-design intent. Google Ads added 174 paid clicks on $486.36 of spend; engagement improved for cpc traffic, but GA4 lead credit still came mainly from organic, direct, and referral paths.

Engagement Context

The July engagement workbook reconciles to 571 US sessions, 385 engaged sessions, and a 67.4% engagement rate. Organic remained the largest and cleanest channel: 283 sessions, 73.9% engagement, 2:40 average engagement time, and 10 GA4 conversions. CPC improved from June to 64.4% engagement on 135 sessions, but still produced only 1 GA4 conversion.

The stronger lower-funnel read came from direct and referral: direct generated 111 sessions, 5:06 average engagement time, and 8 conversions; referral generated 35 sessions and 4 conversions. That makes July a quality-over-volume month rather than a paid-search-led growth month.

Google Ads Context

The MHCC Google Ads account spent $486.36 in July for 174 clicks, 2,316 impressions, a 7.5% CTR, and a $2.80 average CPC. Ads-reported conversions are shown separately from GA4 lead conversions because the ad account's conversion setup counts its own configured actions.

Paid search volume was almost flat with June: spend rose by only $0.57 and clicks rose by 3. The useful change was engagement quality, with GA4 Paid Search engagement rising from 56.0% in June to 64.4% in July. The caution is unchanged: Google Ads reported 274 conversions, but GA4 recorded 23 lead conversions total and only 1 from Paid Search, so these are different measurement systems and should not be blended.

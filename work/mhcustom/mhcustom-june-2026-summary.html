Overview

June brought 656 US sessions, -3.1% from May's 677. Lead-form conversion volume was 29 GA4 conversions, up from 16 in May.

The strongest channel was Organic Search with 358 sessions. Search Console recorded 189 US clicks and 33,343 impressions, with the brand-heavy query set still doing most of the qualified work. Google Ads added 171 paid clicks on $485.79 of spend; the engagement workbook shows that paid traffic helped maintain volume, while organic and referral/direct traffic carried most of the GA4 lead signal.

Engagement Context

The June engagement workbook reconciles to 656 US sessions, 429 engaged sessions, and a 65.4% engagement rate. Organic was the best large-volume medium: 359 sessions, 71.9% engagement, 2:12 average engagement time, and 20 GA4 conversions. CPC delivered 125 sessions at 56.0% engagement and 1 GA4 conversion, so paid search was useful coverage but not the primary source of measured lead actions.

Direct traffic added 118 sessions with 3 conversions, while referral traffic was smaller but productive: 44 sessions, 72.7% engagement, and 4 conversions. That mix argues for keeping paid search active, but reading success through GA4 lead quality and high-intent organic/direct paths rather than through Ads conversion counts alone.

Google Ads Context

The MHCC Google Ads account spent $485.79 in June for 171 clicks, 2,054 impressions, an 8.3% CTR, and a $2.84 average CPC. It reported 241 Ads conversions, which should stay separate from GA4 lead conversions because Ads is using the ad account's configured conversion actions.

The paid-search read is steady but narrow: one Search campaign generated the entire month of spend and traffic. GA4 credits that paid-search traffic with only 1 conversion, so the monthly summary should treat Google Ads as a controlled traffic layer feeding visibility and assisted demand, not as the same thing as the 29 GA4 lead conversions.

Next Month Watch List
1

Keep lead quality ahead of raw traffic volume

June sessions dipped slightly, but conversions rose sharply. The next monthly read should separate qualified lead flow from broad informational traffic.

2

Improve the corner-cabinet CTR opportunity

The corner-cabinet article remains a high-impression page with low click-through. It deserves title/meta and snippet testing before broader content expansion.

3

Keep premium local intent centered

Organic visibility is strongest when MH Custom is framed around high-end custom cabinetry and Mechanicsburg/Harrisburg kitchen design demand, not generic cabinet inspiration.

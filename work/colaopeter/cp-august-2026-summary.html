Executive Readout

Traffic settled after July’s social-heavy peak: August recorded 1,809 sessions, down from 2,786 in July. The drop was mostly lower paid-social volume, while Direct and Organic Search held close to July. With less low-engagement social traffic in the mix, sitewide engagement recovered from 20.3% to 34.2%.

Recommended Next Steps

1

Reconcile form tracking with Ads conversion reporting

GA4 recorded 12 form_submitted events while Google Ads reported zero recorded leads. Confirm the conversion-import and event mapping before using either system to judge paid-search success.

2

Keep paid-social reporting distinct from organic social

August’s 508-session Instagram/Facebook paid stream materially affects both total traffic and engagement mix. It belongs in Paid Social, not Organic Social.

3

Use the detailed reports for diagnosis

This summary is the monthly readout. Use the linked detailed dashboards for search-query, channel, paid-search, and contact-action investigation.

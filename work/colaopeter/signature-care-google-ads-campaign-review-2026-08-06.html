Why This Setup

Search-only, not Performance Max

Google defaults new accounts to Performance Max, which spends across Display and YouTube and uses broad match. At $1,000/month, that would burn the whole budget on the wrong audience. We overrode it to Search-only for tight control.

Custom budget, not Google's suggestion

Google auto-recommended $102.75/day (~$3,082/month) — over 3x the approved budget. We set it manually to $32.89/day to match the $1,000/month ceiling.

Maximize Clicks + $6 CPC cap

Zero conversion history on a brand-new account makes conversion-based bidding unreliable out of the gate. Maximize Clicks with a hard $6.00 CPC ceiling gets qualified traffic in the door without runaway cost-per-click.

3 live targets + expansion queue

The live spend is concentrated on Great Falls, McLean, and Vienna. JR's broader qualified-market list is tracked separately as expansion inventory, not active targeting yet.

Ad Creative Suggestions — Images (2026-08-07)

1. Property-agreement differentiator shot

A C&P crew lead walking an estate property with the homeowner, not a repair-ticket/clipboard vibe — visually separates C&P from the HOA/property-management auction that [property maintenance agreement] currently pulls in.

2. Estate-specific imagery, not generic turf

Large-lot, mature-tree, formal-garden shots specific to McLean, Great Falls, and Vienna properties — with Ashburn, Aldie, Leesburg, Arlington, Oakton, and Clifton ready for future expansion — contrast against the generic mowing/lawn photography used by O'Grady's, A Cut Above, and Angi.

3. Seasonal-care visual sequence

Spring bed reshaping → Summer floral refresh → Fall leaf collection → Winter dormant pruning — mirrors how Google's own AI Overview already frames the "Seasonal Property Care" category to searchers.

4. Brochure-quality stills, not stock photos

Use crops/stills from Brochure_Property-Maintenance_COVER_FINAL_5.29.26.pdf as image extensions — reinforces the "agreement," not "job," framing already in the ad copy.

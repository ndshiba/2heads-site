Why This Setup

Search-only, not Performance Max

Google defaults new accounts to Performance Max, which spends across Display and YouTube and uses broad match. At $1,000/month, that would burn the whole budget on the wrong audience. We overrode it to Search-only for tight control.

Custom budget, not Google's suggestion

Google auto-recommended $102.75/day (~$3,082/month) — over 3x the approved budget. We set it manually to $32.89/day to match the $1,000/month ceiling.

Maximize Clicks + $6 CPC cap

Zero conversion history on a brand-new account makes conversion-based bidding unreliable out of the gate. Maximize Clicks with a hard $6.00 CPC ceiling gets qualified traffic in the door without runaway cost-per-click.

3 live geo targets + broader competitor coverage

The live geography remains concentrated on Great Falls, McLean, and Vienna. Competitor coverage was expanded separately through brand-name conquest ad groups, without broadening the active location targets.

A C&P crew lead walking an estate property with the homeowner, not a repair-ticket/clipboard vibe — visually separates C&P from the HOA/property-management auction that [property maintenance agreement] currently pulls in.

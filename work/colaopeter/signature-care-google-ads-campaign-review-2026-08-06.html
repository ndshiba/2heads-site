Why This Setup

Search-only, not Performance Max

Google defaults new accounts to Performance Max, which spends across Display and YouTube and uses broad match. At $1,000/month, that would burn the whole budget on the wrong audience. We overrode it to Search-only for tight control.

Custom budget, not Google's suggestion

Google auto-recommended $102.75/day (~$3,082/month) — over 3x the approved budget. We set it manually to $32.89/day to match the $1,000/month ceiling.

Maximize Clicks + $8 CPC cap

Zero conversion history on a brand-new account makes conversion-based bidding unreliable out of the gate. Maximize Clicks with a hard $8.00 CPC ceiling gets qualified traffic in the door without runaway cost-per-click.

4-town targeting, not full radius

Rather than spreading a thin budget across the full 40-mile service radius, targeting is concentrated on the four towns with the strongest fit for the estate/high-net-worth buyer.

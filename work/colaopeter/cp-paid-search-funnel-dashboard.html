How to use this page

  • Decision priority: fix or validate paid conversion tracking before changing budget or bidding based on upper-funnel volume.
  • Engaged session: GA4 session lasting 10+ seconds, with a key event, or with 2+ page/screen views.
  • Intent versus lead: Contact-page visits, phone-click users/events, and form starts are intent signals; they are not confirmed leads.
  • Organic comparator: Organic Search uses the same landing-page filter and reporting window. Its small sample must not be treated as a performance trend.

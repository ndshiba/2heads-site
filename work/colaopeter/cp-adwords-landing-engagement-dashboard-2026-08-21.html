Metric Definitions

GA4 Key EventsTimes an event marked as a key event in GA4 fired. This is a tracking designation, not automatically a verified lead.
CTA Button UsersDistinct GA4 users who triggered consultation_click.
Phone UsersDistinct GA4 users who triggered phone_click.
CTA or Phone UsersDistinct users who did either action, with someone who did both counted once.
CTA ClicksTotal consultation_click events. Repeat clicks by one person all count.
Phone ClicksTotal phone_click events. Repeat taps by one person all count.

Do not add CTA Button Users and Phone Users to get a unique-person total; use CTA or Phone Users instead.

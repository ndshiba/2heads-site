How to read this

Actions are not leads. A Contact Us page visit, phone tap, or form start is a useful intent signal, but one person can trigger several actions. The tables therefore do not add up to unique enquiries.

Confirmed form submit is the strongest website signal here. It is emitted from Elementor's form-success signal, unlike the retired generate_lead click proxy that powered the Apr–Jun snapshot.

Executive Readout

September held overall traffic nearly flat at 1,767 sessions, down 2% from August, but the mix shifted sharply. Paid Search grew from 231 to 385 sessions while Paid Social fell from 508 to 320. Sitewide engagement held essentially steady at 34.0%.

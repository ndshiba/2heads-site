What Each Piece Owns

Google Ads owns demand capture

  • Campaign status and budgets
  • Exact and phrase match keywords
  • Negative keywords
  • Responsive search ads
  • Auto-tagged click IDs
  • Imported conversion reporting

The website owns the buyer experience

  • Signature Care landing page
  • Contact CTAs
  • Elementor contact form
  • Phone links
  • Message submission
  • Trust-building page content

GTM and GA4 own measurement

  • GA4 page_view
  • GCLID storage
  • Contact-page view event
  • Phone-click event
  • Elementor form success listener
  • GA4 form_submitted key event
Attribution Flow
Step What Happens Why It Matters
1. Searcher clicks ad Google Ads sends the user to the final URL. Auto-tagging adds a gclid. Campaign #2 also includes manual UTMs. GA4 can attribute the session back to Google Ads, campaign, and click.
2. Landing page loads The Signature Care page returns 200 and loads GTM GTM-KZ6TFDKT plus GA4 G-XBHQWBW7RZ. The visit is measurable before the visitor clicks anything else.
3. Visitor goes to contact The page CTAs lead to the production contact page with ?signature-care=property-walk. The contact-page visit can be tied to the ad session and Signature Care intent.
4. Visitor submits form GTM listens for Elementor success on form c7172e6 and pushes elementor_form_success. The lead event is based on a successful form submission, not just a button click.
5. GA4 records lead GTM fires GA4 event form_submitted, marked as a key event in GA4. GA4 can report lead volume by source, campaign, landing page, and content.
6. Ads imports conversion Google Ads imports www.colaopeter.com - GA4 (web) form_submitted as a primary submit-lead-form conversion. Google Ads can optimize and report against actual website leads.
Tracking Details

Campaign #1 attribution

Campaign #1 uses Google Ads auto-tagging. It does not currently add manual UTMs to the ad final URL, but the account has auto-tagging enabled, so GA4 should classify those clicks through the Google Ads integration and click ID.

Campaign #2 attribution

Campaign #2 uses both auto-tagging and explicit UTMs. Its URL includes utm_campaign=signature_care_competitor_landscape_maintenance and competitor-specific utm_content values, including the Sep 13 additions for Epling, Fine Landscapes, Pristine Acres, Planted Earth, and Green Hill.

How To Read Early Results

In Google Ads

  • Watch Campaign #2 for impression volume across the expanded 11-ad-group competitor set.
  • Watch post-cleanup search terms to confirm the 53 new negatives improve query quality.
  • Check spend split against the $32.89/day combined budget.
  • Use Auction Insights once there is enough impression data.

In GA4

  • Use source / medium, campaign, landing page, and event name.
  • Track form_submitted, contact-page users, form-start users, and phone-click users.
  • Separate Campaign #1 category traffic from Campaign #2 competitor traffic.
  • Expect normal GA4 and Ads conversion-reporting lag.
Known Caveats
The test lead is not a real prospect. The 2026-08-11 Elementor submission proved the conversion chain, but it was a labeled tracking test and should not be counted as a real sales lead.
Competitor volume will still be sparse. Campaign #2 now has 11 enabled ad groups, but competitor-name + maintenance variants are low-volume terms. Many longer variants are rarely served or not eligible; bare brand terms are the practical volume path.
Better CTR/CPC is not enough by itself. September-to-date showed cheaper clicks and stronger CTR, but the upstream search-term mix proved that traffic quality still needed active pruning. The negative list should be judged by whether the next search-term pull looks cleaner, not by CTR alone.
